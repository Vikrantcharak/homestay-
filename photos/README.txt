PHOTOS FOR THE WEBSITE
======================

Put photos in this folder with exactly these names. Each one appears on the
website automatically, replacing the drawing in that spot. Any you don't add
keep showing the drawing, so you can add them one at a time.

  hero.jpg       The big picture at the very top. Wide (landscape). The valley,
                 the river, or the houses from a distance. Your best photo.

  crossing.jpg   MOST IMPORTANT. Someone mid-river in the gharuru, hands on the
                 rope. Upright (portrait). Shoot from the bank so the cable and
                 the water are both in frame.

  old-house.jpg  The old stone house from the outside. Landscape.
  new-house.jpg  The new house from the outside. Landscape.

  clearing.jpg   Both houses in one frame. Square works best.
  inside.jpg     Inside: a bed and a window. Square.
  slate.jpg      The slate roof or old woodwork, close up. Square.

  field.jpg      The field, with the houses or the valley behind it.
  food.jpg       A full plate, shot from directly above, in daylight.
  harvest.jpg    Hands and the harvest. Close up, no faces needed.

TIPS
  • Names must be lowercase and spelled exactly as above.
    .jpg, .jpeg, .png and .webp all work.
  • iPhone "HEIC" photos do NOT work on websites. Send the photo to yourself
    on WhatsApp first; what you download from WhatsApp is a .jpg.
  • Keep each photo under about 500 KB so the site loads fast on mobile data.
    Free tool: https://squoosh.app (drag the photo in, download the smaller one).
  • Visitors can tap any photo to see it full size.
